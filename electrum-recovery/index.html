Electrum Wallet Recovery: Safe Recovery Basics

Recovery is one of the most sensitive wallet operations. Your recovery phrase can control funds, so it should never be sent to a person or entered on a support website.

Use trusted software

Recover a wallet only with trusted wallet software obtained from an official source.

Keep the seed private

Anyone who obtains a valid wallet recovery phrase may be able to control the associated funds. Keep it offline and private.

Beware of support scams

The Electrum project warns that people offering private Electrum support may be impostors. Do not share wallet secrets with anyone claiming to troubleshoot your wallet privately.

Common questions

Is this the official Electrum website?

No. This is an independent educational resource. Use electrum.org for the official project website.

Can I enter my seed phrase here?

No. Never enter or send a seed phrase, private key, wallet file or wallet password to this website.

Where should I download Electrum?

Use the official Electrum project website and the distribution channels it links to.

Does this site provide private wallet support?

No. It provides general educational information and routes users to public official resources.
